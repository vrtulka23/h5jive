# ERA5 sample data licence and attribution

`ERA5_2026_04.nc` is an unchanged copy of the file in the
[ERA5WindRecon example data](https://github.com/DLWangSan/ERA5WindRecon/blob/main/example_data/ERA5_2026_04.nc).
Its attributes identify ECMWF as the data centre and record conversion from
ERA5 GRIB to NetCDF. It is separate from H5Jive's MIT-licensed software.

The [Copernicus Climate Data Store ERA5 single-levels entry](https://cds.climate.copernicus.eu/datasets/reanalysis-era5-single-levels)
lists a Creative Commons Attribution (CC BY) licence. The
[ERA5 documentation](https://confluence.ecmwf.int/pages/viewpage.action?pageId=78293425)
asks for source attribution when distributing the data. Use this notice with
copies of the sample:

> Generated using Copernicus Climate Change Service Information 2026.
> Neither the European Commission nor ECMWF is responsible for any use that
> may be made of the information it contains.
