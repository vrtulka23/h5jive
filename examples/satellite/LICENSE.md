# Data licence and attribution for the GOME-2 sample

The file `gome2_band3_20130106_gome.nc` is a modified extract of EUMETSAT
Metop-B GOME-2 Level 1B data. It is separate from H5Jive's MIT-licensed
software.

**Attribution:** Contains modified EUMETSAT Metop-B GOME-2 Level 1B data
(2013). © EUMETSAT 2013.

EUMETSAT's [Data Policy](https://www.eumetsat.int/legal-framework/data-policy)
is the licence source. In the official [Basic Documents PDF](https://www-cdn.eumetsat.int/files/2026-02/44253%20-%20Basic%20Documents.pdf),
Article 4 classifies Metop GOME-2 Level 1 data as Core data; Article 5 makes
Core data available under [Creative Commons Attribution 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/);
and Article 6 specifies attribution for modified EUMETSAT data. Keep this
notice and the licence link with redistributed copies or images based on the
sample.

The extract contains Band 3 geolocation, pixel bounds, scan direction, and
64 consecutive radiance channels beginning at source channel 480 from
`20130106101404Z_32260_gome.nc` (6 January 2013, orbit 32260). All 625
scan lines and 32 ground pixels are retained; the spectral axis is subset.
It does not contain the complete source product.
